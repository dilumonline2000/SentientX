🧠 AI Career Coach
AI Career Coach is an intelligent, career-support platform designed to streamline your job-seeking process with the power of artificial intelligence. Our system offers a suite of tools to help users stand out in today’s competitive job market:

🤖 AI Mock Interview Questions – Practice with dynamic, role-specific questions to boost your confidence and performance.

✉ Smart Cover Letter Generator – Create tailored, professional cover letters in seconds based on your job role and profile.

📄 AI Resume Builder – Build and enhance your resume with optimized content suggestions and formatting guidance.

📊 Job Insight Module – Get valuable insights into job roles, required skills, and industry trends using real-time data.

Whether you're preparing for your first interview or refining your career strategy, AI Career Coach helps you move forward — smarter, faster, and more prepared.
